## Allowed

- **Unmodified official builds** published by this project may use the
  LoraTraining name and branding.
- Forks and redistributions may **truthfully** state that they are based on
  LoraTraining (for example: “Based on LoraTraining”).
- Quoting the project name when discussing compatibility, history, or
  comparison is fine when it is accurate and not misleading.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Using **LoraTraining**, confusingly similar names, or the official logo as
  the name or primary branding of a **modified** distribution.
- Implying that a fork is the official LoraTraining project, an official
  release, or endorsed by AIPower.
- Using the marks in a way that trades on the official project’s reputation
  while shipping a different product.

## Modified distributions

If you distribute a modified version, choose another product name and branding.
You may still credit the origin under this policy’s “based on” language.
